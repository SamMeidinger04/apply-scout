# Standing policy (sanitized)

- **Apply** matching Calgary / remote-Canada targets in cyber, IT support, junior SWE, and relevant co-ops without asking each time
- **Skip** roles paying roughly $20/hr or less
- **Tailor** resume for strong fits; keep standard one-pager for high-volume Easy Apply
- **Cover letters** whenever the form has a field
- **Escalate** only for puzzle captcha, SSO the candidate must complete, or payment
- **Never** fabricate resume content; never accept interviews or negotiate on the candidate’s behalf
- **Quiet** when a scan finds nothing worth acting on
